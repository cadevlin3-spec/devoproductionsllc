Why Devo?

Devo started with my own wedding.

When my wife and I went looking for a DJ, every quote was more than we could afford. But after years of collecting gear, I realized I already had what I needed. So we did it ourselves for our own wedding.

Then a groomsman asked to borrow my gear for his wedding. I said yes and ran the music for him. After that, a few of the bridesmaids called when it was their turn to get married, and we've never looked back.

I know what it's like to want a great day on a real budget, and that's who I'm here for.

Tell me your date and your budget, and we'll figure it out together.
