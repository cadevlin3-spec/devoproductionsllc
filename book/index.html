Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Send me your date, your venue, and what you're planning, or book a free 30-minute call. I'll come back to you with a quote.

What do you need from the venue?

Two outlets. If the venue doesn't have them, I have a generator and I'll include it in your quote.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. I bring the sound system and the microphones.

How long will you be there?

You have me for the whole day.

How does planning work?

Planning is part of the package. We sit down at least twice before the day so every detail is the way you want it.

Do you travel?

Travel is priced per event, depending on distance. I'll include it in your quote up front.

What kinds of events do you do?

Weddings, birthdays, corporate events, and holiday parties. I also help churches with their sound and train volunteer teams.
